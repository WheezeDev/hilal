Privacy policy — Hilal

Englishภาษาไทย

Effective date: 19 October 2026 Developer: WheezeDev Contact: wheeze.dev@gmail.com

English

Hilal shows prayer times, the Qibla direction and the Hijri date for Muslims in Thailand. It is free, has no ads, and does not require an account.

Location. If you allow it, the app uses your phone's approximate location to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction. This is calculated on your phone. To show the name of your area (for example "Ban Khlong Suan"), your phone's built-in Android place-name service is used; this sends the coordinates to Google, which handles them under Google's privacy policy. If you don't allow location, you can choose your province instead and no location is used at all. The app never uses your location in the background.

What stays on your phone. Your chosen province or last location, language, theme, prayer-time and Hijri settings, and reminder settings are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include these settings in your own Google account backup.

Notifications. Prayer reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

What the app does not do. No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, no crash reporting services, and no selling or sharing of your data. The app does not collect personal information such as your name, contacts, photos or messages.

Children. The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect children's personal data.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact. Questions: wheeze.dev@gmail.com
